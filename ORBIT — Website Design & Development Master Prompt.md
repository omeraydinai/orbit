**29. Business & Brand Architecture**  
**ORBIT is the primary brand**  
ORBIT is the customer-facing brand and the primary identity of the digital platform.  
   
 The website, advertising campaigns, investor journey, qualification system and customer communication should be built around **ORBIT**.  
   
 Do not position PassBeyond as the parent company, umbrella brand, or primary identity.  
**ORBIT's Role**  
ORBIT operates as an **investor acquisition, qualification and advisory layer** connecting international investors with suitable real estate and specialist partners in Greece.  
   
 The core proposition is:  
   
 **Helping international investors find, acquire and secure the right property in Greece while navigating the Golden Visa process.**  
   
 ORBIT owns the investor-facing experience:  
- Lead generation  
- Investor education  
- Golden Visa information  
- Investor qualification  
- Lead capture  
- Investor profiling  
- Consultation booking  
- Investor relationship management  
- Partner coordination  
   
 The long-term vision is to build a structured investor pipeline that can eventually support more sophisticated property-investor matching.  
**Partner Network Model**  
ORBIT should be designed as a **partner-agnostic platform**.  
   
 Different real estate companies, immigration specialists, legal teams, financial advisors and other service providers may work with ORBIT.  
   
 **PassBeyond is one potential partner within this network.**  
   
 Do not make PassBeyond appear to own ORBIT or represent the entire ORBIT ecosystem.  
   
 Conceptually:  
   
                          ORBIT  
   
           Investor Acquisition & Advisory  
   
                               │  
   
              ┌────────────────┼────────────────┐  
   
              │                │                │  
   
         Real Estate      Immigration       Advisory  
   
           Partners        Partners          Partners  
   
              │                │                │  
   
        PassBeyond        Partner B        Partner C  
   
    
   
 The architecture should allow additional partners to be added without changing the core brand identity.  
**Investor Ownership**  
A fundamental principle of the system is:  
   
 **ORBIT owns the investor relationship. Partners provide specialized execution.**  
   
 The investor should experience one coherent ORBIT journey even when different partners are involved behind the scenes.  
   
 The website should therefore avoid language such as:  
- "PassBeyond's properties"  
- "PassBeyond's Golden Visa service"  
- "PassBeyond team" as the default CTA  
- "PassBeyond Greece"  
- "Powered by PassBeyond"  
   
 unless explicitly required for a specific partner implementation.  
   
 Prefer:  
- "ORBIT team"  
- "ORBIT advisors"  
- "ORBIT partner network"  
- "Our local partners"  
- "Our specialist partners"  
**PassBeyond Integration**  
PassBeyond may be introduced where relevant as a specialist partner or service provider.  
   
 If partner attribution is needed, use subtle language such as:  
   
 **Delivered with our trusted local partners.**  
   
 or:  
   
 **ORBIT works with selected real estate and immigration specialists in Greece.**  
   
 If a specific property or service is supplied by PassBeyond, attribution can appear contextually on that individual property/service page.  
   
 Example:  
   
 **Property represented by PassBeyond**  
   
 The partner should never visually overpower the ORBIT brand.  
**Future Expansion**  
The ORBIT architecture should support expansion beyond the initial Greece proposition without changing the core brand.

- Greece Real Estate

- Dubai  
- USA  
- Other investment destinations  
   
 However, **Greece Real Estate + Golden Visa remains the primary proposition at launch.**  
   
 Additional services must remain secondary and should not dilute the initial message.

# **30. UI/UX DIFFERENTIATION — DO NOT DESIGN ANOTHER GOLDEN VISA WEBSITE**  
## **Core Principle**  
The ORBIT website must **not look or behave like a conventional Golden Visa, immigration or real estate website.**  
Visual quality alone is not enough.  
The differentiation must come from the **user experience, information architecture, interaction design and conversion journey.**  
The website should feel like:  
**A digital investment advisory experience.**  
Not:  
A brochure website for a Golden Visa agency.  
The user should not simply browse information.
