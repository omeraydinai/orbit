Greece's residency-by-investment program allows qualifying investors to obtain Greek residency through an approved real estate or capital investment. Current thresholds and requirements are confirmed with you directly by an ORBIT advisor, based on the latest official guidance.

ORBIT operates as a partner-agnostic advisory layer, coordinating with selected real estate, legal and immigration specialists in Greece so you experience one coherent journey from start to finish.
